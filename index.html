      Data quality and method

      Read this before quoting a figure externally.

        Source: Reel Ed delivery data 2026-27-v1.xlsx. Delivery views use sheet "Reel Ed Data", 69 session records covering 3 April 2026 to 1 July 2026. The School grants view uses sheet "EA Access to Angling Fund schls", 14 award records. Every figure is recomputed live from those records, so filtered views always reconcile to the raw data.

        Regions and Environment Agency Areas are derived from the school postcode using the workbook's own Ref-Postcodes and Ref-Counties lookups. Reel Education is funded from fishing licence income through the Environment Agency. Find out more at anglingtrust.net/get-fishing, or join the Angling Trust at anglingtrust.net/membership (01568 620447).

        No pupil-level or personal data appears anywhere in this dashboard. School contact names and email addresses held in the source workbook are excluded. Delivery partner names are shown in a professional capacity only. The "EA Funded School Clubs - Survey" sheet is deliberately not used here; it is case-study and news material rather than dashboard data.
